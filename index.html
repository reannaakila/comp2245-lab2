RickP's Green Acres

RickP's Green Acres is a small, family-owned farm that specializes in organic produce and sustainable farming practices. We take pride in growing fresh fruits and vegetables that are free from harmful chemicals and pesticides. Our farm is committed to providing our community with healthy, locally-sourced food while promoting environmental stewardship.

We offer a variety of fresh produce throughout the growing season, and we are always happy to answer any questions you may have about our farming practices or the products we offer.

CPL 2026

The Caribbean Primier League resulted in a thrilling victory for the Antigua and Barbuda Falcons. As an Antiguan supporter, I was thrilled to see my team claim the championship title after a hard-fought season.

The Falcons' journey to the top was marked by determination, skill, and teamwork. Their performance throughout the league was impressive, and they truly deserved the championship. I am proud to be a fan of such a talented team and look forward to supporting them in future seasons.
